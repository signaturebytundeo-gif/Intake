BROWARD BUILT.

From the Shorelines to the Skylines.

A Declaration of Hometown Pride

BROWARD BUILT. WORLD PROVEN. is a community initiative developed through The World's Brand and operated in partnership with InTake Impact Corp. The program connects community resources, merchandise activations, grants, and strategic partnerships across Broward County's 31 cities through Leadership, Business, and Impact.

Leadership

Recognizing individuals who inspire, mentor, and lead within their communities.

Business

Supporting entrepreneurs and local businesses contributing to Broward County's growth.

Impact

Celebrating individuals and organizations creating positive community change.
